APEX LEGENDS LTM ANALYSIS

2026  |  PERSONAL PROJECT  |  GAME MODE ANALYSIS & LTM CONCEPT  |  RESEARCH & PITCH DECK

I don't just play live-service games. I study why their modes succeed or disappear. This deck scores six Apex Legends limited-time modes on permanence, return frequency, developer signals, and community demand, sorts them into tiers, and pulls out what the winners have in common. Then it puts that analysis to work in an original LTM concept, Re-Kill, where kills earn your team respawns.

Fan analysis and concept, not affiliated with Respawn or EA.

LIVE-SERVICE MODE ANALYSIS

Built a 1-5 scoring rubric (permanence, return frequency, developer signals, community demand) and used public data to rank six Apex LTMs into Gold, Diamond, and Predator tiers, breaking down what worked and why modes did or didn't stay.

ORIGINAL LTM CONCEPT

Designed Re-Kill, a mode where kills fill your team's respawn pool, grounding each rule in the analysis: simplified evo-based loot like WildCard, and fast-paced respawns that match the game's current push toward accessible, casual play.

TEST PLAN & METRICS

Set starting values (a 10 second respawn timer, WildCard-comparable match length), listed the variants worth testing, the metrics to watch, and the open questions the mode needs player feedback on.
