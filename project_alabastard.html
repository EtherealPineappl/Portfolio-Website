ALABASTARD

2022  |  DESIGNER / DEVELOPER  |  UNREAL ENGINE 5  |  1 WEEK BUILD

A metaphysical puzzle game where you solve your own murder after reincarnating as a rock. Built in Unreal Engine 5 in one week for the Unreal MegaJam 2022 — using Metahumans and motion capture to bring the characters to life. A full game loop, a unique premise, and a shipped product in seven days.
